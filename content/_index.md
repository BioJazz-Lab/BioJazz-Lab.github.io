## Welcome

The **BioJazz Lab**, led by [Dr. Song Feng](members/feng/), studies the design
principles of biological systems, from molecular networks to cells and
microbial communities. We ask not only how these systems work, but why they
work the way they do: how evolution shapes them under biophysical and
biochemical constraints, and what design principles it discovers along the way.

We integrate multi-omics measurements, such as proteomics and metabolomics,
with computational modeling. A central goal is to build **mechanistic yet
data-constrained virtual cell models** that capture these principles, so we
can use them to design biological systems, including microbial communities,
beyond what evolution has explored. We also use AI and automation to build
models, tools, and software that accelerate our research.

The lab shares its name with [BioJazz](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkv595),
our software for the *in silico* evolution of cellular networks.
