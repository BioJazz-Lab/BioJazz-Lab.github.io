Why are living systems built the way they are, and how can we design new ones?

We use microbes as tractable, fast-evolving model systems. We pair multi-omics (transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and other phenomics) with mechanistic yet data-constrained virtual cell models, and use AI and automation to speed up the whole cycle from model to experiment.

01Cell Physiology & Communities

How cells allocate limited resources, and how those choices shape microbial communities.

02Predictive Phenomics

Predict how cells respond to genetic, environmental, and drug perturbations.

03Evolutionary Systems & Synthetic Biology

Evolve cellular networks in silico with BioJazz, then design beyond evolution.

04AI for Biology

AI tools linking proteomics to protein structure and function, at scale.
