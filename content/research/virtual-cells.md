Microbial cells must allocate limited resources among growth, maintenance, and
responses to a changing environment. We build **mechanistic yet
data-constrained virtual cell models** that integrate multi-omics data, such as
proteomics and metabolomics, to capture these trade-offs quantitatively, and we
use them to reason about how microbial communities are assembled and how they
can be designed.

Recent work in this area includes multi-omics analysis of the temporal scales
of carbon metabolism in the cyanobacterium *Synechococcus elongatus* under light
disturbance ([PRX Life, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1103/l2dp-kw2t)).
