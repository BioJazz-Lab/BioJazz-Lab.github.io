Bacteriophages reshape host metabolism to support their own replication. We
use genome-scale metabolic models to examine how phage-encoded functions
interact with host metabolism, including synergy and antagonism in
metabolic hijacking ([Science Advances, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeb7646)).
