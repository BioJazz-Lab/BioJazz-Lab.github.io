We are building a collaborative, curious, and supportive team at the interface
of microbiology, systems biology, and computation. Backgrounds in biology,
physics, chemistry, mathematics, computer science, or engineering are all
welcome. What matters most is curiosity and a willingness to learn across
disciplines.

## Graduate students

Prospective Ph.D. students should apply through the
[Baylor Biology graduate program](https://biology.artsandsciences.baylor.edu/graduate-biology-programs)
and mention the BioJazz Lab in their application. Feel free to email Dr. Feng
before applying to discuss research interests.

## Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome inquiries from candidates with experience in computational
modeling, systems biology, multi-omics data analysis, or microbial physiology.
Please email a CV, a brief statement of research interests, and contact
information for references.

## Undergraduate researchers

Baylor undergraduates interested in research for credit, summer research, or
an honors thesis are encouraged to get in touch. Some programming experience
(e.g., Python) is helpful but not required.

## How to get in touch

Email [Song_Feng@baylor.edu](mailto:Song_Feng@baylor.edu) with a short note on
your interests and your CV.
