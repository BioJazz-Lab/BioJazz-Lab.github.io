Zachary Johnson is a Postdoctoral Associate in the BioJazz Lab. Zachary’s
research uses high-throughput experimental analysis and multi-omics to
understand microbial cells at a systems level, with a focus on cyanobacteria:
how gene regulatory networks coordinate day–night metabolic transitions, redox
signaling, and responses to oxidative stress. Zachary has also studied the
assembly and functional specialization of microbial consortia for
lignocellulose conversion.

## Education

- **Ph.D.**, Biological/Biosystems Engineering, Washington State University
  (WSU–PNNL Distinguished Graduate Research Program)
- **B.S.**, Microbiology, San Diego State University
