Song Feng is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biology at Baylor
University. Song’s research program studies the design principles behind
microbial physiology and microbial communities in the context of evolution and
ecology, combining multi-omics approaches, such as proteomics and metabolomics,
with computational modeling. A central goal is to build mechanistic yet
data-constrained virtual cell models for designing microbial communities. Other
projects use AI and automation to build models, tools, and software.

Before joining Baylor, Song was a scientist at Pacific Northwest National
Laboratory and a postdoctoral researcher at the Center for Nonlinear Studies,
Los Alamos National Laboratory.

## Education

- **Ph.D.**, Evolutionary Systems Biology, University of Warwick
- **M.Sc.**, Bioscience, King Abdullah University of Science and Technology
- **B.Sc.**, Biotechnology, Zhejiang University
